Soldier: 76

Soldier: 76 is a versatile damage hero known for his consistent damage output and self-sustain. He excels at mid-range combat and can provide healing to himself and his team with his Biotic Field ability.

Reaper

Reaper is a close-range damage hero who thrives in flanking and ambushing enemies. His shotguns deal devastating damage up close, and his Wraith Form allows him to escape dangerous situations.

Tracer

Tracer is a highly mobile damage hero known for her speed and agility. She can blink through space and time, making her difficult to hit, and her Pulse Bomb can eliminate key targets quickly.
