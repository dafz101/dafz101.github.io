What is a Tank?

Tanks are a crucial role in team-based games like Overwatch, responsible for absorbing damage and protecting their teammates. They have high health pools and abilities that allow them to control the battlefield, create space for their team, and initiate fights. Tanks often lead the charge into battle, drawing enemy fire and creating opportunities for their allies to deal damage.

In Overwatch, tank heroes are designed to be resilient and durable, often equipped with shields or abilities that mitigate incoming damage. They play a key role in maintaining team cohesion and ensuring that their teammates can operate effectively without being overwhelmed by enemy attacks.

Overall, tanks are essential for the success of a team, as they provide the frontline defense needed to secure objectives and protect their allies.

Tank Heroes in Overwatch

Some popular tank heroes in Overwatch include:

  • Reinhardt: A heavily armored hero with a large shield that can protect his team from incoming damage.
  • D.Va: A mech pilot who can absorb damage with her Defense Matrix and has high mobility to engage or disengage from fights.
  • Winston: A gorilla scientist who can leap into the fray, disrupt enemy lines, and create space for his team.
  • Zarya: A tank hero who can absorb damage with her barriers and convert it into increased damage output for her team.
  • Orisa: A robotic tank who can deploy a protective barrier and use her abilities to control the battlefield.

Tank heroes require good positioning, timing, and communication with their team to be effective. They often work closely with support heroes to ensure their survival while maximizing their impact on the game.
