What is a Support?

Support characters in Overwatch are heroes who specialize in healing, buffing, and providing utility to their team. Their primary role is to keep their teammates alive and enhance their performance during battles. Support heroes often have abilities that can heal allies, provide shields, or grant temporary buffs that improve damage output or survivability.

In addition to healing, support heroes may also have crowd control abilities that can disrupt the enemy team, such as stuns or slows. They play a crucial role in maintaining team cohesion and ensuring that the team can sustain itself during prolonged engagements.

Overall, support heroes are essential for the success of a team, as they enable their teammates to perform at their best and contribute to achieving victory.

Support Heroes in Overwatch

Some popular support heroes in Overwatch include:

  • Mercy: A healer who can resurrect fallen teammates and provide continuous healing with her staff.
  • Lúcio: A versatile support hero who can heal allies or boost their speed with his music-based abilities.
  • Zenyatta: A support hero who can heal allies and deal damage to enemies with his orbs, while also providing a powerful ultimate ability.
  • Baptiste: A combat medic who can heal allies, provide temporary shields, and deal damage to enemies.
  • Ana: A sniper support hero who can heal allies from a distance and disable enemies with her sleep dart.

Support heroes require good positioning, awareness, and communication with their team to be effective. They often work closely with DPS and tank heroes to ensure the team's overall success.

Conclusion

In summary, support heroes are vital to the success of a team in Overwatch. They provide healing, buffs, and utility that enable their teammates to perform at their best. Understanding the strengths and weaknesses of each support hero is crucial for players looking to excel in this role.

Interesting Facts about Support Heroes

Here are some interesting facts about support heroes in Overwatch:

  • Support heroes often have unique abilities that can turn the tide of battle, such as Mercy's resurrection or Lúcio's speed boost.
  • Many support heroes have abilities that can heal multiple allies at once, making them invaluable in team fights.
  • Support heroes often require strong game sense and awareness to position themselves effectively and provide maximum benefit to their team.
